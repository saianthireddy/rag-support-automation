# Billing FAQ

## Invoice questions

Invoices are generated on the first business day of each month and cover the
preceding billing period. Customers on annual plans receive a single invoice at
renewal rather than monthly statements.

## Refunds and credits

Refund requests inside thirty days of a charge are approved by the support lead
and processed back to the original payment method within five business days.
Requests outside that window become account credits instead of refunds, and
require finance approval before they are applied.

## Failed payments

A declined card triggers three automatic retries over ten days. After the third
failure the account moves to a grace state; service is not suspended until the
grace period expires. Billing tickets are never escalated to the engineering
on-call rotation.
